## 🎯 Wɔ Nitsumɔhe

Wɔto **Senvon Atelier** shishi yɛ afi 2025 mli, shi nitsumɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ jɛ afi 2023. Afii fioo ni wɔtsu nii wɔ pɛ loo yɛ kuu bibioo mli, kɛha hotel, skul kɛ jarayeli nitsumɔhei — ni wɔkase gbɛ ni wɔtsɔɔ nɔ wɔfeɔ software dani wɔkɛ gbɛi wo nɔ.

Wɔji kuu bibioo ni jɛ **Accra, Ghana**, ni wɔtsuɔ nii kɛjɛɔ he fɛɛ he. Wɔkɛ mɛi ni miite nitsumɔhei shishi kɛ nitsumɔhei bibii fɛɛ tsuɔ nii. Wɔsumɔɔ ni wɔhi bibioo, koni nitsumɔ fɛɛ nitsumɔ aná mɛi ni ná niiashikpamɔ babaoo — ni koni nitsumɔ lɛ afee nɔ ni wɔbaanyɛ wɔkɛ wɔgbɛi awo nɔ kɛ hiɛnyam.

**Nitsumɔhe bibioo ni nyɛɔ efeɔ nɔ ni agbojii feɔ.**

***

## 💎 Nɔ Ni Wɔfeɔ

### **Software Ni Asaa Kɛha Bo** — *Ato yɛ onitsumɔ nɔ*
> *Architecture · APIs · Systems*

Platform kɛ nitsumɔnii ni ato yɛ bɔ ni onitsumɔ lɛ yaa nɔ diɛŋtsɛ nɔ — jeee bɔ ni template susuɔ akɛ eyɔɔ.

***

### **Web Applications** — *Kɛjɛ MVP kɛyashi platform agbo*
> *React · Astro · Cloud*

Web nii ni yaa oya, mɔ fɛɛ mɔ nyɛɔ kɛtsuɔ nii, ni nyɛɔ edaa. Je MVP shishi, ni daa kɛya platform agbo nɔ ni ehe ehiaaa ni ato ekoŋŋ.

***

### **Mobile Apps** — *Code kome, platform enyɔ*
> *iOS · Android · React Native · Flutter*

iOS kɛ Android app ni tamɔ nɔ ni ato yɛ platform lɛ diɛŋtsɛ nɔ, kɛjɛ code kome ni ehe waaa akɛ akwɛ nɔ.

***

### **AI kɛ Machine Learning** — *Nɔ ni damɔɔ mɛi diɛŋtsɛ ahiɛ*
> *LLMs · RAG · Automation*

LLM nii, automation, kɛ data pipeline bibii ni damɔɔ mɛi diɛŋtsɛ kɛ shika ni yɔɔ diɛŋtsɛ ahiɛ.

***

### **Cloud kɛ DevOps** — *Jie nitsumɔ kpo ni osheee gbeyei*
> *Cloudflare · CI/CD · Infrastructure*

Infrastructure ni damɔɔ shi shiŋŋ, CI/CD, kɛ observability, koni release akafee sane agbo dɔŋŋ.

***

### **Product kɛ Design** — *Nɔ ni wa lɛ afeɔ lɛ mlɛo*
> *UX · UI · Design Systems*

Product gbɛtsɔɔmɔ ni niiakasemɔ yeɔ klɛŋklɛŋ, kɛ interface design ni haa nɔ ni wa lɛ tsɔɔ faŋŋ.

***

## 🧪 Wɔ Diɛŋtsɛ Nii

*Jeee wɔ akwadifoi pɛ ahewɔ ni wɔtsuɔ nii.* Kɛji otsuɔ nitsumɔhe nii lɛ, onaa naagbai ni sa akɛ atsa amɛhe shii enyɔ. Nitsumɔnii ni wɔfee kɛha wɔ diɛŋtsɛ ni wɔtsake amɛ nii ni wɔhɔɔ nɛ — wɔkɛ ekome fɛɛ ekome tsu nii yɛ wɔ diɛŋtsɛ nitsumɔ mli dani wɔkɛha mɔ kroko.

### **Home Sweet Home** — *Wɔmiitsu he nii*
> *Software ni yeɔ ebuaa shĩatsɛmɛi kɛ mɛi ni kwɛɔ shĩai anɔ*

Nitsumɔnɔ kome kɛha mɛi ni woɔ shĩai amli, wolo ni akɛfeɔ shĩawomɔ, shika woomɔ, kɛ daa gbi shĩai akwɛmɔ sane.

**Nii ni yɔɔ mli:** API titri ni ato kɛ Go, authentication service ni ato kɛ Hono, TypeScript web client, kɛ Flutter mobile app — wɔgbala amɛ mli koni ekome fɛɛ ekome anyɛ adaa ni anu shishi.

**Nitsumɔhe lɛ etsɔ lɛ diɛŋtsɛ akwadifonyo ni wa fe fɛɛ.**
