## 🎯 Yɛn Adwumayɛbea

Wɔhyehyɛɛ **Senvon Atelier** wɔ afe 2025 mu, nanso adwuma no ankasa fii ase wɔ 2023. Mfe kakra a yɛyɛɛ nnwuma yɛn nkutoo anaa kuw ketewa mu, maa ahɔhoyɛ, nwomasua ne aguadi mu adwumakuw ahorow — na yɛde asuasua yɛn kwan so ansa na yɛde din reto so.

Yɛyɛ kuw ketewa a yɛfi **Accra, Ghana** na yɛn adwuma nyinaa yɛ remote. Yɛne nnipa a wɔrehyɛ adwumakuw foforo ase ne nhyehyɛe nketewa nyinaa yɛ adwuma. Yɛpɛ sɛ yɛtena ase ketewa, sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ a adwuma biara benya adwumayɛfo a wɔanya osuahu kɛse — na adwuma no ayɛ nea yɛde yɛn din bɛto so animuonyam mu.

**Adwumayɛbea ketewa a ɛtumi yɛ nea akɛse yɛ.**

***

## 💎 Deɛ Yɛyɛ

### **Software a Wɔasiesie Ama Wo** — *Wɔsi no wɔ w'adwuma so*
> *Architecture · APIs · Systems*

Platform ne nnwinnade a yɛsi no sɛnea w'adwuma no kɔ so ampa — ɛnyɛ sɛnea template bi susuw sɛ ɛte.

***

### **Web Applications** — *Efi MVP kɔsi platform kɛse*
> *React · Astro · Cloud*

Web nneɛma a ɛyɛ hare, obiara betumi de adi dwuma, na ɛtumi nyin. Fi MVO ase, na nyin kɔ platform kɛse so a ɛnsɛ sɛ wosan si bio.

***

### **Mobile Apps** — *Code baako, platform mmienu*
> *iOS · Android · React Native · Flutter*

iOS ne Android app a ɛte sɛ nea wɔsii no wɔ platform no ankasa so, fi code baako a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔhwɛ so.

***

### **AI ne Machine Learning** — *Nea ɛtumi gyina nnipa ankasa anim*
> *LLMs · RAG · Automation*

LLM nneɛma, automation, ne data pipeline nketewa a ɛtumi gyina nnipa ankasa ne sika a ɛwɔ hɔ ampa ano.

***

### **Cloud ne DevOps** — *Yi adwuma adi a wunnsuro*
> *Cloudflare · CI/CD · Infrastructure*

Infrastructure a ɛgyina pintinn, CI/CD, ne observability, sɛnea release renyɛ ade a ɛyɛ hu bio.

***

### **Product ne Design** — *Nea ɛyɛ den yɛ no mmerɛw*
> *UX · UI · Design Systems*

Product nhyehyɛe a nhwehwɛmu di kan, ne interface design a ɛma nea ɛyɛ den no yɛ pefee.

***

## 🧪 Yɛn Ankasa Nneɛma

*Ɛnyɛ yɛn akwadifo nko ara na yɛyɛ ma.* Sɛ wodi adwumayɛbea a, wuhu nsɛm a ɛsɛ sɛ wosiesie no mprenu. Nnwinnade a yɛyɛe maa yɛn ho na yɛdanee no nneɛma a yɛtɔn ni — yɛde biara dii dwuma wɔ yɛn ankasa adwuma mu ansa na yɛde ama obi foforo.

### **Home Sweet Home** — *Yɛgu so reyɛ*
> *Software a ɛboa adan wuranom ne wɔn a wɔhwɛ adan so*

Nnwinnade baako a ɛhwɛ awɔfoɔ, ntawho krataa, sikatua, ne adan hwɛso mu nsɛm a ɛba da biara.

**Mu nneɛma:** API titiriw a wɔde Go asi, authentication service a wɔde Hono asi, TypeScript web client, ne Flutter mobile app — yɛpaapaee mu sɛnea baako biara betumi anyin na wɔate ase.

**Adwumayɛbea no adan n'ankasa kliɛnt a ɔyɛ den sen biara.**
